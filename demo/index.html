Dead-letter queue

A job lands here after its final allowed attempt. Replay resets the attempt counter and puts it back at the head of the queue — an operator decision, not an automatic retry.

Why jobs die
attempt 4/4

Chaos mode fails 30% of handler runs. With four attempts and full jitter the chance a job exhausts its budget is roughly 0.34 ≈ 0.8% — low, but a fleet processes enough jobs that the DLQ fills anyway.

lease expiry

A worker that stops heartbeating leaves a job in running. The 15-second lease returns it to the queue and spends one attempt, so a job that kills its worker cannot loop forever.

replay

Replay is manual on purpose. Automatic DLQ draining hides the one signal you need: which payloads are permanently broken.
